- Read the whole thing: [Pill to treat pancreatic cancer hailed as ‘biggest breakthrough in decades’ ¦ Pancreas cancer ¦ The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/nov/20/pill-pancreatic-cancer-breakthrough-daraxonrasib)

>In one in 12 of the patients given [the drug, daraxonrasib](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/26/fda-approves-new-pancreatic-cancer-drug), during a trial, the cancer became undetectable, and the tumours stopped growing in almost all of the 61 patients. The trial participants had recently been diagnosed with advanced pancreatic cancer that had spread to other parts of the body.

>Though doctors have said the pill cannot yet be considered a cure, and they do not know how long it can keep the disease at bay, they have said it is encouraging news because it appears it will offer patients more time, even if it does not cause full remission.
