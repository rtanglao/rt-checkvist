- There is a straight line from the 1898 Filipino American war (Sisa) to Marcos's brutal dictatorship (America is not the heart) to 2026 (Filipiñana). There are in fact many such lines. We just have to keep drawing them :-)
- The American men are caricatures but I don't think that's bad thing if you think about it from the perspectives of the Filipinos after years of war. All you can see is the other side's atrocities and stereotypes and in reality, it's much more nuanced than that. I think everybody can agree though that this was a brutal, barbaric, unnecessary war that was the inevitable consequence of Spanish and American colonialism.
- The Filipino women particularly, Hilda Koronel's character,  are real people with real PTSD from years of war.
- Not a hidden war but definitely not something discussed in the Filipino diaspora community. See the links in the next section.
- Wikipedia: [Sisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sisa_(2025_film)), [Hilda Koronel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilda_Koronel), there is no wiki page or website that will persist long-term for America is Not the Heart ([America is Not the Heart on libby](https://share.libbyapp.com/title/3348729), [America is Not the Heart on penguin random house](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/549486/america-is-not-the-heart-by-elaine-castillo/)) so check out the author, [Elaine Castillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elaine_Castillo)
